OUR STORY

Why Rasphera exists

A simple observation about senior isolation in cities, turned into something built with real neighbourhoods.

Founder photo

Rasphera started with a simple observation: in most cities, it's easy for seniors to become isolated even when they're surrounded by neighbours — no shared space, no regular reason to gather.

We wanted to build something that didn't ask seniors to learn new technology to feel connected. So Circles happen in person, on a fixed weekly rhythm, led by people from the community itself.

It started small — a handful of conversations with NGOs already working with seniors, and a willingness to show up in person rather than pitch an app. That's still how every new Circle begins today.
