WHAT WE DO

Small circles, real neighbourhoods

Rasphera runs small, local "Circles" — regular gatherings for seniors to play, connect, and pick up something new. Every Circle is led by a facilitator and rooted in the neighbourhood it meets in.

01

Weekly Circles

Small groups meeting every week for games, walks, or craft sessions — the same faces, the same place, every time.

02

Local facilitators

Every Circle is run by a trained facilitator from the community, not a stranger flown in for the day.

03

NGO partnerships

We work with local NGOs and RWAs already serving seniors, so Circles grow from trusted ground, not cold outreach.
